1) Access & Login

  • Students/parents will receive a login link and/or credentials after registration.
  • If you have not received your login details, contact the school using the form on the Contact Us section.
  • For security, do not share passwords. Teachers will never ask for your password.

2) Announcements

  • School events, reminders, and important notices.
  • Term dates, assembly schedules, and special announcements.
  • How to respond to announcements (via message or form submission).

Tip: Check announcements regularly for updates.

3) Homework / Classwork

  • Weekly homework assignments and classwork materials.
  • Submission deadlines and grading expectations.
  • Recommended learning resources for revision.

Note: If content links are not yet available, teachers will share them in messages.

4) Results & Progress

  • Continuous assessment updates (where applicable).
  • Test scores and term performance summaries.
  • Teacher feedback to support improvement.

5) Communicate With Teachers

  • Send questions or messages to your child’s teachers.
  • Report concerns early for faster support.
  • Request meeting times when necessary.

If the messaging feature is not enabled yet, use the Contact Us form and include your child’s name and class.

6) Support

Need help? Use the Contact form or ask the school office for assistance.

  • Forgot password / account access assistance
  • Updates about login status
  • General platform support
