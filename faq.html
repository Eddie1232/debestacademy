Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions from parents, students, and future applicants. Each question expands with a clear reply to help you navigate school life.

What are the admission requirements?
Admissions require a completed application form, past school records, a birth certificate, and a parent/guardian meeting with admissions staff.
How do I apply for a scholarship?
Scholarship applications are submitted through the admissions office. Awards are based on academic performance, exam results, and financial need.
What are the school hours?
The school day runs from 7:45 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with extra activities offered after school on selected days.
Are uniforms required?
Yes. Students are expected to wear the school uniform every day, including PE days and formal school events.
How do I access the student/parent platform?
Access is granted after registration. Use the parent platform link to log in and view announcements, calendars, and communications.
What is the student-teacher ratio?
Our average class size is about 20 students per teacher to ensure students receive individual attention and guidance.
How can I speak with a teacher?
You can send messages through the platform or arrange meetings during scheduled parent- teacher conferences.
Can I view exam schedules online?
Yes. Exam schedules are published on the Terms Calendar page and are updated by the school administration.
Does the school provide meals?
Nutritious meals are prepared on campus daily, and special dietary needs are accommodated whenever possible.
What happens if my child is absent?
Notify the school office via phone or the contact form as soon as possible. Send a note explaining the absence when your child returns.
How do I request a school transfer?
Contact admissions for the transfer process. You will need to submit records from the previous school and approval from the current and receiving institutions.
Is there a school bus service?
Yes, our school bus service operates on selected routes. Register early to secure a seat and view the service schedule on the platforms page.
How do I pay school fees?
Fees can be paid online, at the school office, or through approved bank deposits. Please keep receipts for your records.
What support is available for new students?
New students receive orientation support, mentoring, and a welcome program to help them settle into school life smoothly.
Do you offer after-school activities?
Yes. We offer sports, music, clubs, and academic support sessions after school.
How is student progress reported?
Progress reports are shared each term through the student/parent platform and parent meetings.
What are the school safety procedures?
We maintain strict safety procedures, including supervised arrival/departure, secure campus access, and regular safety drills.
Can I volunteer at the school?
Yes. Parents and guardians may volunteer for approved school events and PTA activities.
How do I raise a complaint?
Complaints should be submitted through the contact form or directly to the school administration so they can be addressed promptly.
Where can I find school policies?
School policies are available on the school website and through the administration office.
