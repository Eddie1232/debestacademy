Policies and data rights

These plain-language policies explain how DEBEST Academy handles website visitors, enquiries, applications, payments, and school communications. They do not replace any rights provided by applicable law.

Privacy policy

Contact and application details are collected to respond to enquiries, review applications, and administer school services. Student applications must be submitted by a parent or legal guardian. Do not submit sensitive health details or information about other people unless the school has specifically requested it for a current purpose.

Contact form submissions are delivered through Formspree. Application records are stored by the school application service. When the AI assistant is configured, the text of the conversation is sent from the school server to OpenAI to generate a reply; do not enter sensitive or identifying information about a child. The assistant notice links to this disclosure before questions are sent.

Access to application records is role-restricted in the school application. This policy does not publish a specific retention period; contact pta@debestacademy.com to ask about a specific record, request access or correction, or raise a privacy concern.

Terms of service

Use this website lawfully and respectfully. Information is provided for general school communication and may change as the school updates its programmes, dates, or fees. We will identify material changes where practical. Do not submit another person’s information without authority.

Refund policy

This website does not provide online checkout. Before paying the school, request a written, itemised fee schedule and written cancellation and refund terms from the office. Do not rely on an amount or payment instruction that has not been confirmed by the school. For a payment question, use the contact form.

Cookie and browser-storage policy

The current site does not use optional analytics or advertising cookies. The consent banner stores your choice in local browser storage; that preference is not a cookie and does not turn on tracking. Optional tracking remains off. Clear this site’s browser storage to remove the saved choice, or reopen the banner using the settings control.

Accessibility

We aim to support keyboard navigation, visible focus, meaningful labels, alternative text for informative images, and readable contrast. Tell us about an access barrier at pta@debestacademy.com so we can help.

Data deletion request

To ask us to delete personal information, email pta@debestacademy.com with “Data deletion request” in the subject. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information required for safety, accounting, dispute resolution, or law.

Children’s data

A parent or legal guardian must complete a student application and confirm that status before submission. The initial application does not request health records. Children should not submit applications or identifying details directly through the website or assistant.

Email choices and unsubscribe

This website has no newsletter subscription or marketing-email service. Contact-form replies and application communications are service messages. To ask the school to stop nonessential email communication, write to pta@debestacademy.com with “Unsubscribe” in the subject. Any future marketing email programme must include an unsubscribe link before it is launched.

Third-party services and asset rights

Formspree receives contact-form submissions. OpenAI receives assistant conversation text when the AI provider is configured. External social and WhatsApp links take visitors to those providers when clicked. The site source contains no remotely loaded analytics or advertising SDKs and uses system fonts rather than a remote font service.

School photos and the crest are stored locally on this site. Their ownership, releases, and licensing documents are not included in this repository and must be confirmed by the school before publication. Report rights concerns to pta@debestacademy.com.
