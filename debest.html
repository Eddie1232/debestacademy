About DEBEST Academy

DEBEST ACADEMY is dedicated to nurturing growth and excellence in children from their early years (Creche) through Junior High School (JHS). We provide holistic education, balancing academics with character building, arts, sports, and service, ensuring each student’s full potential is realized.

Our Mission

We advance knowledge by creating a conducive environment that equips students with confidence and leadership through quality teaching, care, and morally based training that opens strong academic futures.

Our Vision

To be recognised as an outstanding top private institution offering the highest quality of education with strong religious, moral, and academic training that shapes students for life.

Why parents choose DEBEST

  • Faith-based values and positive discipline
  • Dedicated academic support and mentoring
  • Creative arts, sports, and leadership opportunities
  • Safe, nurturing, and family-oriented environment

Frequently Asked Questions

Browse the most common questions from parents and students. Tap any question to reveal a helpful answer, or use keyboard navigation for fast access.

What are the school hours?
Our daily schedule runs from 7:45 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with after-school activities available on select days.
How do I apply for admission?
Start by visiting the Admission section and completing the online enquiry form. Our admissions team will contact you with the next steps and required documents.
What is the student-teacher ratio?
We aim for a class ratio of about 20 students per teacher so every child receives individual attention and support.
Are meals provided?
Yes, nutritious meals are prepared on campus each day. We also support special dietary requirements when notified in advance.
How can I communicate with teachers?
Use the student/parent platform to send messages, book meetings, or get updates from teachers and administrators.
Where can I find exam dates and term schedules?
Visit the Terms Calendar page for the official exam timetable, term dates, and school events. Approved updates appear there as soon as the Headmaster publishes them.
What should I do if my child is absent?
Notify the school office as soon as possible by phone or the contact form. Regular attendance is important, so please share any medical notes with the administration.
How do I request a school transfer or withdrawal?
Contact the admissions office directly. They will guide you through the transfer or withdrawal process and the required paperwork.
Can I contact the school outside normal hours?
Yes. You can use the contact form or email us anytime. For urgent matters, call during office hours and our staff will help you immediately.
