Contact Us

We are here to help with admissions, school life, and parent support. Reach the office by phone or send us a message below.

How can we help?

Whether you are enquiring about admission, following up on an application, or need help with the student/parent platform, our team will guide you. We typically respond to written messages within 1–2 working days. For urgent matters, please call the office.
