Parent/Teacher Association (PTA)

Connecting Families, Teachers, and the School Community

The PTA helps shape a supportive and welcoming school environment by bringing parents and teachers together to support events, student activities, and community programs.

🤝

Community Spirit

Strengthening connections between families and teachers through collaboration, communication, and shared goals.

🎉

Event Planning

Planning school fairs, sports days, and family gatherings that bring energy and joy to the campus.

📚

Student Support

Supporting academic and emotional growth with programs, workshops, and resources for learners and their families.

Why join the PTA?

Shape school activities

Share your ideas and help create events and programs that matter to students and families.

Stay informed

Receive updates on school news, calendar events, and opportunities for parent and student involvement.

Build relationships

Connect with other parents, teachers, and school leaders to build a stronger school community.
